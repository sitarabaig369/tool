❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save the compressed image?
It's simple — just right-click!

1. Right-click on the preview image above (or long-press on mobile).
2. Choose "Save image as…" from the menu.
3. A dialog opens — browse to any folder you want, rename the file if you like, then click Save.

This works in every browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, Opera) and on mobile too.
Can I choose the folder where the image is saved?
Yes! When you use "Save image as…" your browser opens a native file dialog where you can browse to any folder on your device, rename the file, and confirm. This is the most reliable way to control where the file goes — and it works in every browser.
Is this image compressor really free?
Yes, 100% free. There are no limits, no watermarks, and no account required.
Are my images uploaded to a server?
No. All processing is done entirely in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your images never leave your device.
What is the "Quality Margin Scale"?
It's a tolerance value. If the compressed output would be larger than the original (which can happen with already-optimized images), the tool automatically reduces quality within this margin to guarantee a smaller file size.
Is the output always smaller than the original?
Yes. We guarantee it. If the initial compression doesn't produce a smaller file, the tool automatically reduces the quality (up to your specified margin) until the output is smaller than the original.
